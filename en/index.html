FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What kinds of businesses do you work with?+

Businesses of every size: local shops, sports clubs, cafés, clinics, industrial firms and more.

Is 48 hours really possible?+

Once we have your content and logo, we deliver the first draft within 48 hours. The final version depends on the number of revisions.

Is the demo paid?+

No. The first draft is free; if you don't like it, you pay nothing.

How is the price set?+

By the number of pages, who provides the content and the add-ons you choose. You get a fixed price in writing before work begins.

How does payment work?+

The payment plan is set out in the quote and can be split between the start of work and handover.

Who owns the site and the code?+

You do, entirely. The domain, hosting and code are handed over to you, so you're never tied to us.

Do you buy the domain and hosting?+

We can use what you already have or set it up on your behalf. Either way, the accounts are opened in your name.

Do I get revisions?+

Yes. The number of revisions in your package is stated in the quote, and we make small fixes the same day.

Do you offer support after handover?+

Yes. With a monthly maintenance plan we handle updates and backups, and existing clients can reach us on our WhatsApp support line.

Do you film the videos?+

Videos are made with animation and motion graphics, and we can use footage you shoot yourself. A film crew is a separate service.

Do you work with clients outside Türkiye?+

Yes. We work in Turkish and English and quote international projects in USD or EUR.

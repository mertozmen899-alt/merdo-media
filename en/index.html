Frequently asked questions

If your question isn't here, ask us on WhatsApp.

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

Mostly local shops, cafés, clinics, sports clubs and industrial firms. Small or large doesn't matter.

Is 48 hours really possible?

Once we have your logo and text, we send the first draft within 48 hours. How long the final version takes depends on how many rounds of changes we do.

Is the demo paid?

No. The first draft is free. If you don't like it, you don't pay anything.

How is the price set?

We look at the number of pages, who writes the content and which add-ons you want. You get the price in writing before we start.

How does payment work?

The payment plan is in the quote. If you like, we can split it between the start of work and handover.

Who owns the site and the code?

You do, entirely. The code and domain are yours and the hosting is handed over to you too. You're never tied to us.

Do you buy the domain and hosting?

We can use what you already have or set it up on your behalf. Either way, the accounts are opened in your name.

Do I get revisions?

Yes. The number of revisions is in the quote. Small fixes we usually make the same day.

Do you offer support after handover?

Yes. On a monthly maintenance plan we handle updates and backups. Existing clients can message our WhatsApp support line.

Do you film the videos?

We usually make videos with animation and motion graphics. We can also use footage you shoot yourself. A film crew is a separate service.

Do you work with clients outside Türkiye?

Yes. We work in Turkish and English and quote international projects in USD or EUR.
